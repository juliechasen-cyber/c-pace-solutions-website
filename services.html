Transactions

Six ways C‑PACE fits into a deal

The structure changes with the project. The goal does not: the lowest-cost, longest-dated capital available for the eligible scope.

New Construction

Fund the energy, water and resiliency scope of a ground-up build. C‑PACE takes a slot in the stack that would otherwise need equity, so the deal needs less cash to close.

Best when The project is in design or early construction and the equity check is the constraint.

Renovations & Retrofits

HVAC, building envelope, lighting, water systems, roofing, elevators and seismic work on an asset you already own.

Best when Core building systems are aging out and the capital plan is stretched.

Retroactive C‑PACE

Recover capital from eligible improvements already completed. Each program sets its own lookback window, so timing matters.

Best when A recent project was funded with equity or short-term debt.

Recapitalization

Replace short-term, floating or higher-cost debt with long-dated, fixed-rate capital. Restore debt service coverage and extend the runway.

Best when A loan is maturing, or mezzanine or preferred equity is costing more than the asset can carry.

Building Repositioning

Change what a building is. Office-to-residential conversion, tenant reconfiguration, or a full systems replacement that resets the asset for its next life.

Best when A change of use or major scope makes the business plan depend on low-cost capital.

Bridge Financing

Fill a timing gap in the stack while the project stabilizes or a permanent takeout comes together.

Best when The takeout is lined up but the closing date is not.

Eligible Items

What C‑PACE can pay for

Typical measures funded by C‑PACE. The exact eligible list is set by each state program, so scope is confirmed before anything is placed.

Energy Efficiency

  • HVAC systems
  • Boilers & chillers
  • Building automation & control systems
  • LED lighting
  • Building envelope, including insulation, windows, doors & roofing
  • Motors & drives

Renewable Energy

  • Solar and solar thermal
  • Fuel cells
  • Cogeneration
  • Geothermal
  • Wind

Water Efficiency

  • Low-flow plumbing fixtures
  • Irrigation sensors and controls
  • Greywater and wastewater recovery
  • Water-efficient appliances (permanently affixed)
  • Rainwater harvesting

Resiliency

  • Seismic
  • Wind-hardening
  • Microgrids
  • Storm water management systems
Property Types

Commercial assets across the board

C‑PACE can fund projects across a wide variety of commercial property types. It follows the building, not the sector.

Office

Base-building retrofits, tenant reconfiguration, and conversions to residential or mixed use.

Industrial

Roofing, lighting, refrigeration and building systems for warehouse, distribution and cold storage.

Mixed-Use

Buildings combining retail, office and residential, where one assessment can cover shared systems.

Multifamily

In-unit and central HVAC, water fixtures, envelope and common-area systems across garden, mid-rise and high-rise assets.

Hospitality

Hotels carry heavy HVAC, hot water and lighting load. Systems upgrades and renovation scope often qualify.

Retail

Shopping centers and street-level retail, from envelope and lighting to full systems replacement.

Healthcare

Central plant, HVAC and resiliency systems for medical office, assisted living and senior housing.

Nonprofit

Tax-exempt owners can use C‑PACE where tax credits and depreciation offer them nothing.

Not on the list? Ask. Eligibility is set by each state program, and other commercial property types may qualify.
