What Is C‑PACE

Long-term, non-recourse capital for your property

Commercial PACE financing funds eligible energy efficiency, water conservation, resiliency and building-envelope improvements, repaid through a voluntary assessment on the property. It is fixed-rate, non-recourse, and underwritten to the asset, not a personal guarantee. Here's where it fits:

New Construction

Fund the energy, water and resiliency scope of a ground-up build and reduce the equity the deal needs to pencil.

Renovations & Retrofits

HVAC, envelope, lighting, water systems, roofing, elevators and seismic work on an asset you already own.

Retroactive C‑PACE

Pull capital back out of eligible improvements already paid for, subject to each program's lookback window.

Recapitalization

Replace short-term, floating or higher-cost debt with long-dated fixed-rate capital and restore coverage.

Building Repositioning

Change what a building is. Office-to-residential conversion, tenant reconfiguration, or a full systems replacement.

Bridge Financing

Fill a timing gap in the stack while the project stabilizes or a permanent takeout comes together.

The Process

How a C‑PACE assessment comes together

  1. Eligibility review

    We confirm the property sits in an active program district and identify which improvements in your scope qualify.

  2. Structure & place

    We size the assessment, take it to the capital providers most active in that program, and coordinate mortgage lender consent.

  3. Program approval

    The application goes to the program administrator, documents are executed, and the assessment is recorded.

  4. Fund & repay

    Capital funds at closing or through construction draws. Repayment arrives as a line item on the property tax bill.

Why C‑PACE

Capital structured around your property

C‑PACE sits in its own place in the capital stack, and that changes what a deal can carry.

100% financing

The eligible scope is funded in full, with no out-of-pocket contribution at closing.

Fixed for up to 30 years

Long amortization holds annual payments low and keeps them out of the rate cycle.

Non-recourse

The assessment runs with the property. No personal guarantee, no corporate guarantee.

Transfers on sale

The obligation conveys with the asset, so you only pay for the years you hold it.

Displaces costlier capital

Steps into the slot mezzanine or preferred equity would hold, at a materially lower cost.

Lifts NOI and value

Lower utility spend and modernized systems show up directly in net operating income.
